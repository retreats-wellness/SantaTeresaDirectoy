The Nicoya Peninsula

One of the world's
five Blue Zones.

The Nicoya Peninsula is one of only five places on earth where people consistently live past 100. The lifestyle here — fresh food, daily movement, strong community, and a deep connection to nature — is the reason. Santa Teresa is at the heart of it.

This directory covers Santa Teresa, Mal Pais, Playa Hermosa, Cabuya, Manzanillo, and Montezuma — the full southern tip of the peninsula.

The Area

Five villages.
One peninsula.

Santa Teresa
The main hub. Surf, restaurants, yoga, nightlife, and the best waves on the peninsula. Playa Carmen runs south.
Mal Pais
Just south of Santa Teresa. Quieter, rockier, rawer. Tide pools, local fishing, and a strong expat community.
Playa Hermosa
North of Santa Teresa. Long empty beach, consistent breaks, and a more local crowd. Sunsets are exceptional.
Cabuya
Gateway to Cabo Blanco Reserve. Small fishing community with a mysterious island cemetery. Off the beaten path.
Manzanillo
Remote village inland. Organic farms, permaculture projects, and a strong community of growers and naturalists.
