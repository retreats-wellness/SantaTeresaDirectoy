The Nicoya Peninsula

One of the world's
five Blue Zones.

The Nicoya Peninsula is one of only five places on earth where people consistently live past 100. Fresh food, daily movement, strong community, and a deep connection to nature. Santa Teresa is at the heart of it.

The Area

Five villages.
One peninsula.

Santa Teresa
The main hub. Surf, restaurants, yoga, nightlife, and the best waves on the peninsula.
Mal Pais
Just south of Santa Teresa. Quieter, rockier, rawer. Tide pools and a strong expat community.
Playa Hermosa
North of Santa Teresa. Long empty beach, consistent breaks. Sunsets are exceptional.
Cabuya
Gateway to Cabo Blanco Reserve. Small fishing community with a mysterious island cemetery.
Manzanillo
Remote village inland. Organic farms, permaculture projects, and a strong community of growers.
